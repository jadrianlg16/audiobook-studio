Chapter One: The Lighthouse

Rain hammered the windows as Elara climbed the last of the stairs. The lamp room was dark and cold.

"You let it go out," she said.

Marcus did not look up. "The oil ran dry an hour ago," he answered. "There was nothing I could do."

"Then we signal by hand," Elara replied. "Fetch the mirrors."

He finally turned to face her. "In this storm? No ship will ever see us."

"They will see us," she said. "Because we are going to make them see."

Marcus was quiet for a long moment. "You always did believe in impossible things."
