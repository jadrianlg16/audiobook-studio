Chapter One: The Cold Lantern

The storm had been promised for three days, and on the third it finally came. Dr. Vane climbed the tower stairs with the slow patience of a man who had climbed them ten thousand times before. At the top, the great lamp waited, unlit.

"You knew the light would go out," Elara said. She did not turn from the window.

"I knew nothing of the sort," he answered. It was 4:15 in the morning, and the sea was the color of iron.

* * *

By dawn the wind had fallen. Marcus set down the cold lantern and said nothing at all. There was nothing, he thought, that words could mend.

Chapter Two: A Borrowed Name

They left the island on the first clear tide. No one watched them go.
