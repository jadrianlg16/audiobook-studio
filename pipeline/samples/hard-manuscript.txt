Chapter One: The Ledger

It was 1943, and Dr. Aoife Ni Bhraonain had 3.14 reasons to lie.[1] The ledger showed 1,204,553 francs missing from the St. Mary's account (see Ch. 4).

"Someone has been careless," said Ren.

Alex shrugged. "Nobody saw him leave."

Sam laughed. "You're all being ridiculous."

Priya turned. "Colonel Vasquez."

Vasquez waited until the room went quiet. He did not sit.

"Sit down," he said.

"Am I?" The voice came from the doorway.
